# v2.4.0 - June 02, 2026

This release introduces a complete cart redesign with new conversion-focused features, a refreshed Nook preset, built-in AI shopping agent compatibility, 8 new sections and blocks, expanded button animations, performance optimizations, and accessibility improvements across the theme.

## Highlights

- **Cart Redesign** — new tiered discount progress bar, paid or free-gift services checkbox, enhanced cross-sell visibility, and refined drawer interactions
- **Nook Preset Refresh** — refreshed navigation styling, updated demo templates, and improved visual rhythm
- **AI & Commerce Protocol Compliance** — structured data support for AI shopping assistants
- **8 New Sections and Blocks** including Timeline, Contact Form with Image, Pills Navigation, Product Categories Pills, and Compact Rich Text
- **Performance & Mobile LCP Improvements** — PDP image preload, instant.page link preloading, and overall page-speed optimizations
