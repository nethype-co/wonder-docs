# v2.5.1 - August 21, 2026

A refinement release that builds on 2.5.0 with smoother storefront interactions, better performance, stronger SEO, and broader compatibility across the theme.

## Highlights

- **More Polished Product Page** — the Add to Cart button responds instantly to variant selection, and the gallery and header settle cleanly on load
- **Lighter Content-Heavy Pages** — sliders and background videos release resources when no longer needed
- **Stronger Blog SEO** with modern structured data

## Changed

- **Add to Cart** — the buy button now reflects your variant choice immediately, without needing a page refresh, including when "preselect first variant" is turned off
- **Product Page Loading (Fashion Layout)** — the media gallery and transparent header settle smoothly on first load, without a visible shift or flash
- **Quick Buy** — now available on Pro product cards in Product Recommendations, and on Quiz result cards
- **Video Controls** — play/pause and mute controls display and behave correctly in the Video section
- **Featured Collection** — smoother reveal animation for the stacked / grid view
- **Multicolumn on Mobile** — two-column layouts align correctly on smaller screens
- **Quiz** — the results slider behaves consistently across screen sizes

## Performance

- Sliders and videos now fully release their resources when a section is removed or re-rendered (for example, while editing in the theme customizer), and off-screen videos are paused, keeping content-heavy pages responsive

## SEO & Structured Data

- Blog articles now provide complete, standards-based BlogPosting structured data (JSON-LD), helping search engines better understand and surface your posts
