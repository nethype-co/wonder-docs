## Scroll to top

The **Scroll to top** setting adds a floating button that lets customers quickly jump back to the top of the page, improving navigation on long pages. These settings are found under **Theme Settings** → **Scroll to top** in the Theme Editor.

## Settings

- **Enable scroll to top button:** Enable this toggle to display the scroll to top button on your store.
- **Button color style:** Choose the visual style of the button — **Primary** or **Secondary**.

## Position

- **Horizontal spacing:** Set the distance between the button and the edge of the screen.
- **Align to left side:** Enable this toggle to position the button on the left side of the screen. Overrides the right-side position and places the button on the left.
